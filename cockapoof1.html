Mini F1 Cockapoo Puppies

Meet our current mini F1 Cockapoo litter, raised right here in London, Kentucky. Our Cockapoo puppies are family-raised, have wavy, low-shedding coats, and are known for their friendly, people-loving personalities. To see more examples of past litters, visit our Cockapoo litters page, and for details on how everything works, read our buying process page.

These puppies were born on November 30, 2025. They have been de-wormed on schedule starting at three weeks, received their first vet check and age-appropriate shots around six weeks of age, and are ready to go home now. Message us to set up a pickup time (or ask about delivery options).
